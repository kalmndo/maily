# Maily — Design Context

## Target audience
Personal users running their own email client. One person, their own inbox.

## Use cases
Reading, composing, replying to, and organizing email. Primary daily-driver email interface.

## Brand personality
**Modern, playful, fun.** Clean and confident like Linear or shadcn — near-black sidebar, near-white background, electric blue accent. High contrast, no fluff.

## Color palette
- **Background**: `hsl(0 0% 99%)` — near white
- **Foreground**: `hsl(240 10% 4%)` — near black (zinc-950)
- **Primary / accent**: `hsl(217 91% 60%)` — electric blue (compose button, active nav, unread indicators, focus rings, starred emails)
- **Sidebar**: `hsl(240 6% 10%)` — dark zinc, near black
- **Sidebar text**: `hsl(240 5% 65%)` — muted zinc
- **Sidebar active**: blue on dark zinc
- **Borders**: `hsl(240 6% 90%)` — zinc-200
- **Muted surfaces**: `hsl(240 5% 94%)` — zinc-100

## Typography
- **UI sans**: Instrument Sans (400/500/600) — warmer and more distinctive than Inter, applied via `font-sans`
- **Display serif**: Lora (400/600/700) — applied via `font-serif` to reading pane subject lines and the "Maily" login wordmark
- **Weights**: 400 body, 500 labels/medium, 600 headings/semibold — nothing heavier needed
- **Reading pane subject**: `font-serif text-2xl font-semibold leading-snug tracking-tight`
- **Timestamps**: always `tabular-nums`
- Font variables: `--font-sans`, `--font-serif` (set by Next.js `next/font/google`)

## Layout
3-pane email client: dark zinc sidebar (224px) → email list (320px) → reading pane (flex-1).

## Anti-patterns to avoid
- Warm-tinted neutrals (this is a cool/neutral zinc palette)
- Purple-blue gradients
- Navy and gold (old palette — replaced)
- Glassmorphism
- Generic card grids in admin panel
